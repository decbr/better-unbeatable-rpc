    New style

    Drag pieces into a line, and click one to change it.

    How pieces work

    A piece is left out when its information is missing, along with the text before and after it, so a song with no artist doesn't end in a stray " - ". It can show something else instead, like "No PB yet". Clicking a piece in the palette adds it to the end of the highlighted line.

    Click a placeholder to insert it at the cursor.

    How templates work

    Text in [square brackets] is left out when a placeholder in it is empty, and [a|b] shows b instead. Write \[ \] \{ \} \| or \\ for the character itself.
